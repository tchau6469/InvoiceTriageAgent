# Prism Analytics Platform Order

This synthetic order covers hosted business-intelligence software.

## Platform pricing

The platform fee is $2,425 per month and includes 40 named users. Additional
users cost $35 per user per month.

## Seat expansion

Up to ten additional users may be added with service-owner approval. Eleven or
more additional users require a signed order form and PO.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must state the service month, base fee, and count of added users. The
approved cost center is `DATA-PLATFORM`.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 45. Invoice language stating shorter terms does
not supersede the contract.

## Term and renewal

The term ends December 31, 2026 and does not renew without written approval.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes require vendor-management verification. Payment remains a
human decision.
