# Signal Media Buying Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers approved digital-media placements.

## Media spend

The monthly media-placement cap is $10,000 unless a signed insertion-order
amendment authorizes more. The agency fee is 8% of actual media spend.

## Evidence

Invoices must include campaign code, channel, run dates, actual media spend,
agency fee, and publisher or platform delivery report.

## Credits

Publisher rebates, make-goods, and invalid-traffic credits must be passed
through in full and identified as negative line items.

## Purchase orders

Every campaign requires a PO and approved insertion order. Spend may not be
moved between campaigns without written marketing approval.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 45.

## Remittance and authority

Payment-detail changes and payment approval require human verification.
