# Redline Courier Services Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers local document and small-parcel delivery.

## Delivery rates

Local-zone same-day delivery is $32 per shipment. Metro-zone same-day delivery
is $48. Rush service may add 50% to the applicable base rate.

## Included charges

Fuel, standard proof of delivery, and one delivery attempt are included. A
second attempt may be charged at 50% of the base rate.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must list tracking number, pickup date, origin zone, destination zone,
service level, and requestor. A tracking number may be billed only once.

## Purchase orders

A PO is required when a department's weekly courier charges exceed $1,000.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 15.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payment approval require human review.
