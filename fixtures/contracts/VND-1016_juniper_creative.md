# Juniper Creative Studio Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers recurring design and campaign support.

## Monthly retainer

The retainer is $4,800 per month and includes up to 35 design hours, two review
rounds per deliverable, stock assets, and project administration.

## Additional work

Approved additional design time is $135 per hour. More than ten extra hours in
a month requires a signed change order and PO.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must state the service month, included hours, additional hours,
campaign code, and change-order reference when applicable.

## Prohibited charges

Internal meetings, ordinary file storage, and account-management fees are not
separately billable.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payment decisions require human approval.
