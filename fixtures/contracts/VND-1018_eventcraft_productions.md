# EventCraft Productions Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers corporate-event production services.

## Production fees

Venue and production coordination is $6,500 per event. On-site event staff are
$65 per person-hour. Each event has a four-hour minimum per staff member.

## Rentals and pass-through costs

Approved equipment rentals may be billed at documented cost plus no more than
8%. Catering, venue, and travel costs require receipts and may not be marked up.

## Authorization

Every event requires a unique event code and PO. Costs above the event PO need
a signed amendment before they are incurred.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must list event code, event date, venue, staff count and hours,
rental receipts, pass-through receipts, and PO.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30 after event-owner acceptance.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and all payment actions require human approval.
