# Harbor Legal Operations Engagement

This synthetic engagement covers contract-review support and legal operations.

## Hourly rates

Attorney time is $310 per hour and paralegal time is $160 per hour. Clerical
work, research databases, and ordinary copying are included.

## Monthly cap

Fees may not exceed $8,500 per month without written approval from General
Counsel and a PO amendment.

## Invoice requirements

Each line must include matter ID, professional role, service date, hours, and a
non-privileged task description. Block billing is not permitted.

## Expenses

Travel requires prior approval. Pass-through expenses above $75 require a
receipt and may not include markup.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 45 following legal-department review.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payment actions require independent human approval.
