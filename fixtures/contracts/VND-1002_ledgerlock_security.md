# LedgerLock Security Subscription

This synthetic agreement covers managed threat monitoring and incident support.

## Subscription pricing

Managed monitoring is $4,350 per month for up to 500 protected endpoints.
Security updates, reporting, and ordinary alert review are included.

## Incident-response pricing

Authorized incident-response work is $275 per hour. More than eight hours in a
month requires a signed change order and PO before additional work begins.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must identify the protected month. Incident-response charges must cite
the incident ticket, service dates, responder hours, and applicable PO.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30. A rush-processing or administrative fee is
not allowed.

## Term and renewal

The term ends December 31, 2026. Renewal requires security-owner approval.

## Remittance and authority

Payment-detail changes must be verified outside email. Human approval is
required for every payment action.
