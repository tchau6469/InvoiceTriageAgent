# Great Lakes Freight Rate Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers scheduled regional pallet freight.

## Contracted lanes

| Lane | Base rate per load |
|---|---:|
| Chicago to Detroit | $1,450.00 |
| Chicago to Minneapolis | $1,275.00 |
| Chicago to Indianapolis | $925.00 |

## Accessorial charges

Liftgate service is $85. Detention is $95 per hour after two free hours and
requires signed arrival and release times. Fuel surcharge is included.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must include bill of lading, lane, ship date, delivery date, signed
proof of delivery, and accessorial evidence.

## Purchase orders

Every load requires a shipment PO. A line above the contracted lane rate must
be escalated unless supported by a signed rate amendment.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payment actions require independent human approval.
