# Clearwater Facilities Services Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers janitorial and common-area maintenance.

## Recurring services

| Location | Monthly fee |
|---|---:|
| SITE-HQ | $3,200.00 |
| SITE-ANNEX | $1,650.00 |
| SITE-WH | $1,000.00 |
| Total | $5,850.00 |

Supplies, equipment, supervision, and ordinary travel are included.

## Emergency services

Authorized emergency labor is $145 per hour. Materials may be billed at cost
plus no more than 10% and require receipts above $100.

## Purchase orders and invoicing

Every invoice requires a PO. Emergency work also requires a work-order number.
Only one recurring invoice per site and month is permitted.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30. Late fees are prohibited.

## Service credits

An uncorrected missed service earns 5% of the affected site's monthly fee.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and all payment decisions require human verification.
