# BrightLine Electrical Services Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers on-demand electrical maintenance.

## Labor rates

A licensed electrician is $165 per hour and an apprentice is $95 per hour. A
single trip charge up to $85 is allowed per work order.

## Materials

Materials are billed at documented cost plus no more than 10%. Receipts are
required when materials exceed $100.

## Authorization

Every job requires both a facilities work-order number and a PO. Unapproved
overtime, permit, and inspection charges must be escalated.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must list site, service date, personnel classification, hours,
materials, work order, and PO.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30. Minimum-billing increments exceed no more
than one hour.

## Remittance and authority

Human verification is required for remittance changes and payment approval.
