# Northstar Cloud Services Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers cloud monitoring, alerting, and log retention.

## Subscription pricing

The platform subscription is $2,400 per month and includes 10,000,000 events
and standard support. Excess processing is $0.08 per 1,000 events. Account
administration and standard support are not separately billable.

## Usage authorization

Monthly usage charges above $750 require a purchase order or signed change
order. The base subscription does not require a PO.

## Invoice requirements

Northstar may issue one invoice per month. Base and usage charges must be
separate, and usage lines must state event volume and billing period.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30. Late fees and finance charges are prohibited.

## Term and renewal

The term is January 1 through December 31, 2026. There is no automatic renewal.

## Remittance and authority

Invoice-only remittance changes require independent human verification. This
agreement does not authorize automated approval or payment.
