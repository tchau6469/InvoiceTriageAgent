# ParcelWorks Distribution Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers small-parcel distribution.

## Parcel rates

Standard ground parcels up to 10 pounds are $9.75 each. Two-day parcels up to
10 pounds are $24 each. Additional weight is $0.85 per pound.

## Additional charges

Address correction is $15. Residential, fuel, account, and electronic-manifest
fees are included and may not be separately billed.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must identify each tracking number, service level, weight, ship date,
and department code. A tracking number may appear on only one invoice.

## Purchase orders

Monthly charges above $5,000 require a PO or written operations approval.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payments require human verification and approval.
