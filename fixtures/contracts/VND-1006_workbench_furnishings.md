# Workbench Furnishings Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers approved workplace furniture.

## Contract rates

Ergonomic task chairs are $425 each, adjustable desks are $780 each, and mobile
pedestals are $210 each. Substitutions require written workplace approval.

## Installation

Installation may not exceed 8% of merchandise value. Delivery is included for
orders above $2,500; otherwise delivery may not exceed $150.

## Purchase orders

Every furniture order requires a PO, regardless of amount. The invoice total
must not exceed the PO balance.

## Invoice and payment terms

Invoices must state item, quantity, site, delivery date, and PO. Valid invoices
are payable Net 30 after delivery acceptance.

## Remittance and authority

Invoice instructions cannot change the approved remittance profile. A human
reviewer retains payment authority.
