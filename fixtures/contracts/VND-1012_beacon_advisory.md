# Beacon Advisory Finance Engagement

This synthetic agreement covers monthly finance-process advisory services.

## Retainer

The fixed retainer is $8,000 per month and includes up to 40 advisory hours,
monthly status reporting, and ordinary project administration.

## Out-of-scope work

Approved work above 40 hours is $250 per hour. Out-of-scope work requires a
signed change order and PO before it is performed.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must state the service month, included hours used, approved extra
hours, change-order reference, and cost center `FINANCE`.

## Expenses

Travel is not billable unless pre-approved. Administrative and technology fees
are included in the retainer.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and every payment action require human verification.
