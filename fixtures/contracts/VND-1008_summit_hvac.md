# Summit HVAC Maintenance Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers preventive and emergency HVAC service.

## Preventive maintenance

Quarterly preventive maintenance is $3,200 and covers all three company sites.
Filters, inspection labor, and ordinary travel are included.

## Emergency work

Emergency labor is $185 per hour. Materials may be billed at documented cost
plus 12%. Overtime requires prior written approval.

## Purchase orders

The quarterly fee uses standing PO `PO-2026-2208`. Emergency and repair work
requires a separate PO and service-ticket number.

## Invoice requirements

Invoices must identify the site, equipment asset, service date, labor hours,
materials, and applicable PO.

## Payment terms

Valid invoices are payable Net 30. Diagnostic fees are not separately billable.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes and payment approvals require human review.
