# Meridian Office Supply Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers ordinary office consumables.

## Contract rates

| SKU | Description | Maximum unit price |
|---|---|---:|
| PAP-A4-10 | Recycled copy paper, 10-ream case | $46.00 |
| PEN-BLK-12 | Black gel pens, box of 12 | $14.50 |
| NOTE-3X3-12 | Repositionable notes, pack of 12 | $11.25 |
| TONER-MONO-X | Monochrome toner | $89.00 |

## Shipping and fees

Ground shipping is free at $250 or more. Shipping below that threshold may not
exceed $18. Handling, account-management, and convenience fees are prohibited.

## Purchase orders

Orders above $1,000 before tax and shipping require a PO. Orders may not be
split to avoid the threshold.

## Invoice and payment terms

Invoices must list SKU, quantity, unit price, requestor, and PO when required.
Valid invoices are payable Net 45.

## Remittance and authority

Remittance changes require independent verification. The agent cannot approve
or issue payment.
