# Cedar Paper Products Agreement

This synthetic agreement covers bulk paper products.

## Contract rates

Letter-size recycled paper is $41 per 10-ream carton. Legal-size recycled paper
is $49 per 10-ream carton. Whiteboard pads are $28 per two-pad pack.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is free for orders of $300 or more. Smaller orders may include
delivery up to $20. Fuel and warehouse fees are not permitted.

## Purchase orders

Orders over $1,500 before tax require a PO. Repeated invoices for the same
packing slip must be treated as possible duplicates.

## Invoice and payment terms

Invoices must include SKU, quantity, packing-slip number, and delivery date.
Valid invoices are payable Net 30.

## Term

The agreement expires December 31, 2026 with no automatic renewal.

## Remittance and authority

Banking changes require human verification. Contract terms do not confer
payment authority.
