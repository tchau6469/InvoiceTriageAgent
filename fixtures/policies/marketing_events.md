# Marketing and Events Spending Policy

## MEP-01 — Campaign or event code

Every marketing invoice must include an approved campaign or event code. The
code must map to the billed cost center.

## MEP-02 — Purchase orders

Media placements and events require a PO. Creative retainers follow their
vendor-specific threshold for additional work.

## MEP-03 — Media caps

Media spend must remain within the insertion order and monthly contract cap.
Excess spend requires a signed amendment.

## MEP-04 — Agency fees

An agency fee must be calculated from actual eligible media spend using the
contract percentage. Pass-through costs are excluded unless expressly stated.

## MEP-05 — Delivery evidence

Media invoices require platform or publisher delivery reports. Event invoices
require event-owner acceptance and service evidence.

## MEP-06 — Creative retainers

Retainer work may not be billed again as hourly work. Extra hours must show the
campaign code and required change authorization.

## MEP-07 — Event staffing

Event staff invoices must show headcount, hours, dates, and contracted rate.
Minimum-hour rules apply per person, not to the event as a whole.

## MEP-08 — Rentals and pass-through costs

Rental markup may not exceed the contract percentage. Venue, catering, travel,
and other pass-through charges require receipts and may not be marked up unless
the contract allows it.

## MEP-09 — Credits and make-goods

Publisher rebates, make-goods, cancellation credits, and invalid-traffic
credits must be passed through and shown as negative invoice lines.

## MEP-10 — Budget coding

Campaign spend normally uses `MARKETING`; event production normally uses
`EVENTS`. A different cost center requires written allocation evidence.

## MEP-11 — Remittance security

Last-minute remittance changes in creative, media, or event invoices require
independent vendor verification.

## MEP-12 — Human approval

The agent may not approve a campaign, event, invoice, or payment. Final action
requires an authorized human reviewer.
