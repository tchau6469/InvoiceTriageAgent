# Facilities Maintenance Spending Policy

## FMP-01 — Site identification

Every invoice must identify the serviced company location. Charges lacking a
site code must be reviewed.

## FMP-02 — Work orders

On-demand and emergency work requires a facilities work-order number. The
agent may not infer or generate a missing work order.

## FMP-03 — Purchase orders

Use the vendor-specific PO rule. Where a contract requires every invoice to
carry a PO, no dollar-value exception applies.

## FMP-04 — Recurring service periods

Each site and recurring service period may be charged only once. Overlapping
periods or repeated site-month combinations are possible duplicates.

## FMP-05 — Labor rates

Labor classification, hours, and rate must be itemized and compared with the
contract. Unsupported overtime premiums require escalation.

## FMP-06 — Material markup

Materials require evidence at the contract threshold and may not exceed the
vendor-specific markup cap.

## FMP-07 — Included costs

Supplies, filters, supervision, diagnostics, or travel included in a recurring
fee may not be invoiced separately.

## FMP-08 — Emergency documentation

Emergency charges must show service date, location, work performed, labor, and
the required PO and work-order references.

## FMP-09 — Service credits

Apply a contractual missed-service credit only when supported by operational
evidence. Do not invent or estimate a credit.

## FMP-10 — Budget availability

Compare the full invoice with the remaining monthly facilities budget. An
otherwise valid invoice that exceeds remaining budget requires review.

## FMP-11 — Remittance security

Invoice-level banking or remittance changes require independent vendor
verification before any payment recommendation.

## FMP-12 — Human approval

The agent cannot approve work, create missing authorization, or issue payment.
An authorized facilities or finance reviewer makes the final decision.
