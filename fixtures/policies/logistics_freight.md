# Logistics and Freight Spending Policy

## LFP-01 — Shipment identity

Every charge must include a unique tracking number, bill of lading, or shipment
identifier. Missing identifiers require review.

## LFP-02 — Duplicate shipment charges

A shipment identifier may be paid only once. Reused tracking or bill-of-lading
numbers are possible duplicates even when invoice numbers differ.

## LFP-03 — Contracted lanes

Freight origin, destination, and base rate must match an approved lane. A rate
above the lane price requires a signed amendment.

## LFP-04 — Service level

Courier and parcel charges must identify service level. Rush or expedited
premiums are allowed only within the applicable contract.

## LFP-05 — Weight validation

Weight-based charges must show billed weight and the contractual base allowance.
Unexplained weight adjustments require review.

## LFP-06 — Accessorial evidence

Detention, liftgate, address-correction, and redelivery charges require the
evidence and rate specified in the contract.

## LFP-07 — Included fuel

Fuel surcharges are prohibited when the vendor contract includes fuel in its
base price. The invoice may not override that term.

## LFP-08 — Proof of delivery

Freight invoices require signed proof of delivery when stated by contract.
Missing proof prevents a clear recommendation.

## LFP-09 — Purchase orders

Apply shipment-level, weekly, or monthly PO rules exactly as written in the
vendor contract. The agent may not create missing authorization.

## LFP-10 — Billing period

Charges must fall within the invoice period and active contract term. Old
charges require explanation and duplicate review.

## LFP-11 — Payment terms

Use vendor-contract payment terms. Courier Net 15 terms must not be generalized
to freight vendors whose contracts state Net 30.

## LFP-12 — Human approval

The agent may flag and recommend but cannot authorize a shipment expense or
execute payment.
