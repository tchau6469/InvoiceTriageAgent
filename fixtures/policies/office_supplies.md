# Office Supplies Spending Policy

## OSP-01 — Approved goods

Consumable supplies and contract-listed workplace goods are allowed. Gift
cards, personal items, and unapproved electronics are prohibited.

## OSP-02 — Rate-card validation

SKU, quantity, and unit price must be checked against the vendor rate card.
Substitutions above the approved price require written approval.

## OSP-03 — Purchase-order threshold

Apply the vendor contract's PO threshold to merchandise value before tax and
shipping. A vendor-specific all-orders rule takes precedence.

## OSP-04 — Split transactions

Related orders or invoices must not be divided to avoid a PO or approval
threshold. Suspected splitting requires human review.

## OSP-05 — Shipping

Shipping must comply with the vendor's free-shipping threshold and maximum fee.
Unsupported fuel, handling, or warehouse charges are not payable.

## OSP-06 — Delivery evidence

Invoices must include a delivery date, packing slip, or acceptance reference
when required by contract. Missing evidence is a review condition.

## OSP-07 — Furniture acceptance

Furniture invoices are eligible only after documented delivery acceptance.
Installation must remain within the contractual percentage cap.

## OSP-08 — Duplicate shipments

An invoice number, packing slip, or shipment identifier may not be paid twice.
Corrected invoices must identify the original record they replace.

## OSP-09 — Returns and credits

Returns must be credited at the original unit price. Restocking charges are
allowed only when the contract expressly permits them.

## OSP-10 — Payment terms

Use the contract payment terms even if the invoice requests earlier payment.
Do not treat a due date as a contractual amendment.

## OSP-11 — Budget coding

Ordinary supplies use `CORP-ADMIN`; furniture uses `WORKPLACE` unless a written
allocation provides another cost center.

## OSP-12 — Human approval

No invoice, contract, or model output constitutes payment approval. An
authorized employee must make the final decision.
