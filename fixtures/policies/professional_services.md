# Professional Services Spending Policy

## PSP-01 — Active engagement

Services require an active vendor and unexpired engagement. Post-termination
charges require a new agreement or documented extension.

## PSP-02 — Scope validation

Invoice descriptions must map to contracted milestones, retainers, or approved
hourly work. Vague descriptions require review.

## PSP-03 — Milestone acceptance

Fixed-fee milestones are eligible only after the acceptance evidence named in
the contract. Partial billing is prohibited unless expressly allowed.

## PSP-04 — Hourly detail

Hourly invoices must state service date, role, hours, rate, and meaningful task
description. Block billing is not acceptable.

## PSP-05 — Retainer limits

Work included in a retainer may not be billed again. Hours above a retainer
allowance require the contractually specified authorization.

## PSP-06 — Fee caps

Monthly or engagement fee caps must be enforced before budget analysis. Charges
above a cap require a signed amendment or change order.

## PSP-07 — Expenses

Travel and pass-through expenses require prior approval when stated by contract.
Required receipts must be present, and markup is prohibited unless contracted.

## PSP-08 — Matter and project codes

Legal matters, advisory engagements, and technology projects must carry the
applicable matter, engagement, or project identifier.

## PSP-09 — Inactive vendors

Historical records remain searchable for audit and duplicate detection. An
inactive vendor must not be treated as approved for new work.

## PSP-10 — Payment terms

Use the executed agreement's payment terms. Invoice requests for earlier
payment do not override the agreement.

## PSP-11 — Confidentiality and logs

Reasoning traces must not reproduce privileged narratives or unnecessary
personal data. Record only the minimum evidence needed for the decision.

## PSP-12 — Human approval

An agent recommendation cannot accept a milestone, approve an engagement, or
authorize payment. Those actions remain with designated humans.
