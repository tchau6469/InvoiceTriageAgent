# Cloud Software Spending Policy

## CSP-01 — Approved cost center

Cloud invoices must use the cost center assigned in the vendor master or an
approved project cost center. A vendor category does not itself establish
budget approval.

## CSP-02 — New subscription threshold

A new subscription or expansion with annualized value above $10,000 requires a
PO and written approval from the service owner.

## CSP-03 — Usage overages

Usage-based charges must remain within the contract cap. Excess charges require
the PO, change order, or other authorization named in the vendor contract.

## CSP-04 — Automatic renewals

Automatic-renewal language does not establish internal approval. Renewal
invoices require documented service-owner approval.

## CSP-05 — Unsupported fees

Setup, account-management, convenience, administration, and standard-support
fees are not payable unless expressly priced in the contract.

## CSP-06 — Billing period

A recurring service month may be invoiced only once. Overlapping billing
periods must be checked for duplication before recommendation.

## CSP-07 — User and seat counts

Seat-based invoices must state current seat count and added seats. Counts above
the contracted allowance require the approval specified in the contract.

## CSP-08 — Service credits

Contractual uptime and service credits must be applied as negative line items.
Missing expected credits require review rather than agent calculation.

## CSP-09 — Taxes

Sales or use tax must appear separately and be consistent with the service and
jurisdiction. The agent must not invent a tax amount.

## CSP-10 — Payment terms

The contract's payment terms prevail over shorter terms printed on an invoice.
Discrepancies must be recorded in the reasoning trace.

## CSP-11 — Remittance changes

Banking or remittance changes require independent vendor verification. Email or
invoice instructions alone are insufficient.

## CSP-12 — Human approval

The agent may recommend pay, hold, or reject, but may never approve, schedule,
or transmit a payment.
